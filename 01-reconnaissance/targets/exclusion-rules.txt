# OUT OF SCOPE - NEVER REPORT THESE

## Critical Exclusions (HackerOne Core)
- Scanner-generated reports (Retire.js, Vega, Nessus, OpenVAS, Prowler, Qualys)
- Self-XSS that cannot be exploited on other users
- Known vulnerable libraries without working POC
- Attacks on X-Bug-Bounty header or FIND_ME cookie
- Email or user enumeration
- Missing autocomplete attributes for forms

## Program-Specific Exclusions
- Social engineering (phishing, vishing, smishing)
- Physical access attacks
- EOL browsers or platforms (Internet Explorer, etc.)
- Google Maps API key exposure (already known)
- Prismic Access Tokens (read-only, public, intended)
- Wayback Machine findings without current exploitation proof
- Vulnerabilities affecting EOL browsers

## Ineligible Assets
- legal.clearme.com (None severity)
- hub.clearme.com (None severity)
- fieldsupport.clearme.com (None severity)
- docs.clearme.com (None severity)
- clearme.atlassian.net (External platform - report to Atlassian)
- Physical devices (airports, stadiums, etc.)

## Testing Restrictions
- Do not brute force rate-limited APIs
- Do not mass create accounts (max ~12 accounts recommended)
- No damage/disruption to systems
- Only test accounts you own or with written permission
- First reporter wins on duplicates
